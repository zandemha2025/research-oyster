RESEARCH OYSTER

Capture settings

Pair this browser with the Oyster service running on your computer. Pairing codes are single-use and the resulting token stays in extension storage.

Local connection

Only localhost and 127.0.0.1 are accepted.

Privacy defaults

Captured candidates remain local until you click Approve & save. Automated redaction cannot find every identity; review and edit every item before saving.

What this extension never does

It does not read passwords or cookies, bypass access controls, join communities, run while stopped, or upload candidates without your approval. Only collect information you are allowed to access and use.
